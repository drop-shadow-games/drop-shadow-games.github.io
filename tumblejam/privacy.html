Tumble Jam – Privacy Policy

Drop Shadow Games · Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Tumble Jam (the "game"), made by Drop Shadow Games ("we", "us"). The short version: the current version of the game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, coins, boosters, skins and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

What we collect

Nothing. The current version of the game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal data, and we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, Google handles the download and any purchases under Google's Privacy Policy. If you choose to share crash reports or app diagnostics with developers in your Android settings, Google may give us anonymous crash and performance reports. They do not identify you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that use data (for example ads, in-app purchases, analytics or cloud saves), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
